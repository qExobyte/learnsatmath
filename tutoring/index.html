Common questions

If your SAT is less than 2 weeks away → Buy the Jumpstart Plan to prep intensively.

If a live Masterclass cohort is starting soon (or has just started) and you're scoring above a 630 → Join the live Masterclass.

If neither of the above cases are true, and you're willing to commit time, effort, and money to improve your score, then pick the coaching package that fits your goal — or book a free call and we'll figure out the right one together.

With personalized coaching, you'll be provided a concrete list of tasks between each session.

These include:

  • SAT Math Masterclass recordings, covering the concepts and problems in-depth
  • Bluebook tests with specific score range targets for each test
  • Focus areas – Tasks assigned based on your weaknesses from the last session.
  • 130+ of my own proprietary practice problems

The custom plan schedules tasks based on their expected duration to complete, keeping you on track with your expected weekly prep hours for your goal score.

As soon as you purchase a plan, you'll get a Calendly link to book your sessions in any open time slot.

There's no need to lock in all your session times up front — each session is yours to book whenever it works for you. Just note that all sessions in your plan must be used by September 30, 2026.

A simple Google search for "Princeton Review tutor salaries" shows they pay tutors $20–$40/hr while charging $250/hr+. You're paying for the brand, not the tutor.

Their bundles (up to $10,000!) prioritize quantity over quality — hundreds of unengaging videos and thousands of problems, many AI-generated. But students only have so much time.

My tutoring is focused on efficiency and outcomes. At any point in our prep, we focus entirely on the topic that yields the highest return in the lowest amount of time.

I do not offer score guarantees. Almost every test prep platform with score guarantees has caveats in the fineprint (i.e. "must complete all 500 hours of material"). I don't want a score guarantee to relieve students of accountability or disincentivize perseverance.

However, my study time calculator is based on data from over 100 students across the masterclass and 1-on-1 sessions. You'll get a reliable metric for how many hours per week are needed to reach your goal score, and there will be periodic Bluebook tests to ensure you're on the right track.

Sessions are fully reschedulable as long as you cancel at least 6 hours before the scheduled start time. This can be done automatically through Calendly or by sending an email. Otherwise, the session will be counted towards the plan's total.

Sessions in coaching packages may also be rescheduled at least 6 hours before the scheduled start time. Please reach out with any questions specific to your package.
