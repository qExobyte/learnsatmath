Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before enrolling.

The curriculum can be followed without purchasing any additional resources. However, I highly recommend that students purchase a plus subscription for CookSAT. It costs $24/mo and includes hundreds of challenging practice problems with high quality desmos solutions.

While all Masterclass students are aiming for top scores, some students want to push harder than others. So, I've developed two distinct plans:

The Standard Plan is around 4-5 hours a week outside of classes, for students aiming for a 700-760 / generally an 80-100 point improvement.

The Advanced Plan is around 6-8 hours a week outside of classes, for students who are gunning for a perfect score.

Both plans include a detailed list of homework assignments for each week, so students know exactly what to work on.

The Masterclass is designed for students currently scoring between a 600 and 750, who are aiming for a 750-800. If you're below a 600, you can still join, but you'll need to work extra hard to keep up.

No worries! Every session will be recorded, so you'll never get behind. If you have questions from the recording, shoot me an email.

The Masterclass has a hard cap of 15 students. It's much lower than most test prep, because I want the class to feel like a discussion, not a lecture.

Office hours are a chance for students to get personalized help with any topics/problems they're struggling with, live in a Google Meet call with other students. Sometimes, students don't have any particular questions, but still tune in to the call in case anything interesting comes up!

Schedules aren't always predictable this far in advance! If I ever need to cancel a session due to sickness or major events, we'll take a poll and reschedule to the time that works best for everyone.

I do not offer score guarantees. Almost every test prep platform with score guarantees has caveats in the fineprint (i.e. "must complete all 500 hours of material").

Instead, I let the results of my students speak for themselves. Nearly every Masterclass student who has put the requisite time and effort to get a 750 has achieved it. I don't want a score guarantee to relieve students of accountability or disincentivize perseverance.

Within 7 days from the first session, you're able to request a full refund of the masterclass, no questions asked. If you get a refund, you'll lose access to the class's recordings and resources.
